The Transactional Email Kit is defined as: Eight production HTML lifecycle emails: welcome, receipt, password reset, trial ending, payment failed, digest, closing, and reactivation for Gmail/Outlook.

Every SaaS needs the same eight lifecycle emails, and every founder ships them as an afterthought: broken tables, image-only receipts, dunning copy that reads like a threat. This kit is the set, table-coded with inline CSS and a send map.
