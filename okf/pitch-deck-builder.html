The Pitch Deck Builder

By Akshat Naruka · Updated 2026-11-17 · Category: CAREER & TEACHING · Format: 16 SLIDES + PPTX

The Pitch Deck Builder is defined as: A 16-slide PPTX deck template with a worked seed-stage example, speaker notes, and a walkthrough that answers one investor question per slide.

Thirty-slide decks get skimmed, and decks without an ask get filed. The ten-slide structure exists because each slide has one job; this builder gives you those ten plus six appendix slides, filled with replaceable example copy.

What's inside

  • A 16-slide PPTX: ten core slides plus appendix (pipeline, cohorts, pricing, benchmark definitions, FAQ, contact)

  • Speaker notes written as the words to say, not bullet reminders
  • A PDF walkthrough: the one question each slide answers, the make-or-break rule, and the numbers-honesty block
