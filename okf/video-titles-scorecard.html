Titles & Hooks Scorecard

By Akshat Naruka · Updated 2026-11-17 · Category: GETTING CUSTOMERS · Format: 6 CHECKS · 30 PTS

Titles & Hooks Scorecard is defined as: An XLSX scoring workbook for video titles and hooks: six checks scored 0 to 5, twelve title patterns, twelve hook patterns, and a weekly ship log.

Titles written in the eleven minutes before upload get judged by the person who least qualifies: you, who already knows the video. The scorecard replaces feel with a rubric and compares every upload against your channel median.

What's inside

  • The Scorer sheet: 20 rows, six checks (front-load, curiosity, specific, honest, readable, length), auto verdicts
  • A Pattern Bank of 12 title patterns with the job each does
  • A Hooks sheet pairing each title pattern with a first-line hook and a worked example
  • A ship log that reads views against the channel median, not your best day
