Notion Meeting OS

By Akshat Naruka · Updated 2026-11-17 · Category: OPERATIONS · Format: NOTION · MEETINGS

Notion Meeting OS is defined as: A Notion meeting system: notes, decisions, and owner-assigned actions, with a 15-minute Friday review that closes every loop the week opened.

Meeting notes die in a docs folder and actions die in the notes. The next meeting restarts from zero because nobody can find what was decided last time. This system gives every meeting continuity: what happened, what was decided, who owns what.
