SaaS Playbooks, Self-Paced Courses & Founder Operating Systems

By Akshat Naruka · Updated 2026-11-17 · 69 systems live

Probiex playbooks and self-paced courses are defined as evidence-backed, execution-first operational systems for solo SaaS founders, indie hackers, and freelancers. Unlike 20-hour video courses, every playbook opens with cited industry data (such as 98% of live vibe-coded apps carrying a vulnerability or 59% of small-business invoices running 30+ days late) and hands over the working templates, XLSX models, Notion schemas, or CLI scripts to fix the problem in one afternoon.

How to choose the right playbook or course

  • Building with AI and about to charge money: Start with The 15-Point Vibe-Code Safety Check, The Vibe-Code Rescue Kit, The Trust & Legal Pages Pack, The Security Questionnaire Vault, and AI Agent Production System.
  • Getting B2B customers and organic distribution: Run The X / Twitter Growth Playbook, Compliant LinkedIn Automation Stack, Cold Email Deliverability & DNS Kit, and The Warm Outbound Engine.
  • Fixing leaky signups, offers, and 1099 cash flow: Use SaaS Activation & Retention OS, Offer & Pricing Stress Test, and Freelance 1099 & Tax Planner.
  • Designing and teaching a course or cohort: Build with The Course Builder for Notion and AI Literacy Engineering Kit.
